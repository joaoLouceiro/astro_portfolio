## Welcome

Hey, there!

Welcome to my humble corner of the internet.

My name's João, and I have been developing software professionally since 2018.

I have mainly worked as a Full Stack developer, using Java and Spring Boot for back-end programming, JavaScript and SAP UI5 for front-end, and SAP BTP for all cloud-related mumbo-jumbo.

I eventually grew tired of SAP's ultra-opinionated world, and decided to take a break. I took the opportunity to really dig deep into Linux, distributed systems, and wrapping my head around low-level programming.

This website comes out of a need to share my findings with the outer world.

So what do I have to offer that's different than most other tech blogs out there? Maybe nothing. But this is my bet: given my trajectory into the dev world, I have a good know-how knowledge base, but I miss a lot of the "givens" that most resources out there have.

So I'll try to cover the basics as much as possible, without detouring from the main subject... Well, as much as I can... I tend to get into big segues...

## About this website

I have written the whole website using AstroJS.

I only wanted to try out the framework, but I ended up enjoying it so much that here we are. It is a breeze to work with. I suggest going over [their documentation](https://docs.astro.build/en/concepts/why-astro/). They say it better than I could.

Most of this website was done without AI, although I do use it to help out with the translation of the blog posts.

The color-scheme is based on Everforest. You can find more about it [here](https://jamesatk.in/everforest-homepage/).

Anyway, welcome.
